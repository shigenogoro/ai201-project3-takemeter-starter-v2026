analysis: makes a claim and backs it with at least one specific, checkable fact: a stat, a transaction, a roster detail, or a named source
hot_take: takes a position someone could argue against (a prediction, a ranking, a verdict on a player or trade) and offers nothing checkable to support it
reaction: emotional venting (cheering, frustration, disgust, relief) about a play, a result, the news, or the league, with no argument behind it
joke: a pure bit written to get a laugh (an absurd hypothetical, a fake quote, a meme format) with no real complaint or claim underneath
